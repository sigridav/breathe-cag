## Summary

Asthma and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) share type 2 inflammatory mechanisms, but little is known about early signs that predict who will develop them. This project examines whether reduced sense of smell and elevated blood eosinophil counts at baseline predict later diagnoses of asthma and/or CRSwNP, using survival analysis. No new biological material is collected. The results may support earlier diagnosis and more targeted treatment of type 2 airway disease. The project is a PhD project.
