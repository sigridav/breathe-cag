## Summary

COPD is often diagnosed late, when much of the small airways is already lost, and there are no established ways to identify people who are on their way to developing the disease. A recent study (Sauler et al., Nature Genetics 2026) described a pattern of inflammatory blood proteins linked to COPD disease processes. This project uses HUNT participants with blood samples and spirometry from before and after COPD diagnosis to test whether this protein pattern is present before disease onset, to study its genetic determinants, and to use Mendelian randomisation to assess causality. The aim is biomarkers of early COPD risk. No new data are collected.
