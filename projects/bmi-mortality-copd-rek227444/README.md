## Summary

WHO recommends a BMI of 18.5–25 in adults, but in COPD low BMI is associated with higher mortality and higher BMI with lower mortality (the obesity paradox). This project examines all-cause and cardiovascular mortality across BMI levels with Cox regression, and compares the BMI with the lowest mortality in people with and without COPD, to inform whether BMI recommendations should differ in COPD.
